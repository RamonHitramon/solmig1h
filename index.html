Test rules

Test: SOL MIG1H M1 · Filter: SOL · MIG1H · Test tag: #solmig1hm1

Filter determines eligible SOL migration-age signals. Age at migration must be at least 1h. It is the native GMGN completion time minus creation time, with open-market listing confirmed. Tokens that migrated younger are excluded even if they are older now. There is no market-cap threshold. Strategies are exit rules. Test SOL MIG1H M1 compares six independent virtual accounts.

Free SOL is spendable virtual cash, not the marked value of open tokens. Account equity includes open-position marks.

Strategy activity follows the public snapshot.

Per strategy: 1.000 SOL, entry 0.100 SOL including modeled costs, reserve 0.010 SOL. Only migrations completed after this test starts are eligible; missing or inconsistent timestamps are rejected. Entry requires a fresh migration (within 180 seconds), not merely a new observation of an old token. Duplicates are not bought after a restart.

TP20 / TP25 / TP30 / TP35 / TP40: +20 / +25 / +30 / +35 / +40% on the full-position sell quote in SOL relative to the purchase amount before added fees and gas (not net ROI). No initial stop-loss, trailing stop, time-based exit or end-of-day exit. Positions are held until TP. This is an independent M1 test; the earlier experiments are preserved separately.

TP30BE15 strategy: a gross +15% gain in the full-position sell quote relative to the purchase amount arms a fixed modeled net break-even stop. TP30 remains active. This is not a trailing stop. The stop is checked on subsequent sampled quotes; a gap can produce a negative result, which is not forced to zero. All accounts start fresh for M1; no trades or histories are imported.

Quotes are read-only ExactIn for the actual modeled trade size. The demo uses conservative minimum output with 0.5% slippage. Additional modeled costs are a 1% fee on each side and 0.00005 SOL gas per operation; these are assumptions, not paid expenses. DEX fees are already included in the quote. No transactions are signed: actual execution, MEV protection and sellability are not guaranteed.

Monitoring cycle: 5 seconds plus request time; evenly paced queue with at least 1.3 seconds between requests and a shared persistent budget of 48 quotes/min. Shared watch limit: 20 tokens across all strategies; long-held positions in one strategy can limit new entries in others. Jupiter server key is enabled: Free plan 60/min per organisation; local shared cap 48/min leaves headroom. The monitoring-regime change is recorded in the run; Price/hybrid triggers remain disabled. API rate-limit headers are respected. Peaks between observations may be missed. No quote means no invented trade. Old marks are flagged. The public snapshot updates about every 2 minutes; this page checks once per minute.
